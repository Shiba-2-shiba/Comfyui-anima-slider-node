Slider LoRA 概念候補カテゴリ
1. 表情・感情系

最も作りやすいカテゴリです。顔の変化として出やすく、portrait / close-up / upper body で評価しやすいです。

smile_intensity_slider	big smile, cheerful expression	neutral expression, relaxed face	高	口の開き slider にならないよう注意
emotion_valence_slider	bright happy expression, cheerful mood	gloomy expression, melancholic mood	高	怒り・泣き顔を混ぜない
eye_openness_slider	wide open eyes, alert eyes	half-closed eyes, relaxed eyelids	高	感情を固定する
eyebrow_angle_slider	arched eyebrows, sharp eyebrows	soft eyebrows, relaxed eyebrows	中	怒り表情に吸着しやすい
blush_intensity_slider	visible blush, flushed cheeks	clear cheeks, no blush impression	中	恥ずかしさや性的文脈に寄せない
calm_intensity_slider	calm expression, serene face	tense expression, alert face	中	表情が弱くなりすぎる可能性
mouth_openness_slider	open mouth, speaking expression	closed mouth, relaxed lips	中	smile と混ざりやすい
sleepy_expression_slider	sleepy eyes, drowsy expression	awake eyes, alert expression	中	目の開き slider と近い

smile_intensity_slider、eye_openness_slider、emotion_valence_slider

2. 顔パーツ・顔形状系

アニメ系モデルと相性が良いカテゴリです。ただし cute や young に吸着しやすいので、adult face、neutral expression、same hairstyle 的な共通条件を target 側に入れると安定しやすいです。

jaw_sharpness_slider	sharp jawline, defined facial contour	soft jawline, rounded facial contour	中高	性別印象に混ざる可能性
cheek_fullness_slider	full cheeks, soft cheeks	slim cheeks, defined cheeks	中	顔の丸さと近い
nose_prominence_slider	prominent nose, defined nose bridge	small nose, subtle nose	中	アニメモデルでは効きが弱い場合あり
chin_length_slider	longer chin, defined chin	short chin, soft lower face	中	顔全体の印象が変わりやすい
facial_softness_slider	soft facial features, gentle face	sharp facial features, defined face	中	抽象的なので分解候補
mature_face_slider	mature facial features, adult face	soft youthful facial features	低中	年齢方向に寄るため慎重に扱う

おすすめ初回候補:
eye_size_slider、face_roundness_slider、jaw_sharpness_slider

Local LLM に提案させる用途なら、候補カテゴリを次のように定義すると安定します。

## Concept categories

Use these categories when proposing new Slider LoRA concepts.

1. Expression sliders
   - Best for first experiments.
   - Use portrait, close-up, upper body.
   - Keep hairstyle, outfit, background fixed.
   - Examples: smile_intensity, eye_openness, emotion_valence.

2. Face shape sliders
   - Useful for character design.
   - Avoid age-related wording.
   - Use adult, neutral expression, hair away from face when needed.
   - Examples: eye_size, face_roundness, jaw_sharpness.

3. Hair sliders
   - Usually effective because hair tags are strong.
   - Do not mix hair color, length, curliness, and volume in one slider.
   - Examples: hair_length, hair_volume, hair_messiness.

4. Clothing and fabric sliders
   - Practical and safer than body sliders.
   - Put clothing category in target; change only modifiers in positive/unconditional.
   - Examples: clothing_fit, fabric_weight, outfit_decoration.

5. Body proportion sliders
   - Useful but higher risk.
   - Always use adult, fully clothed, nonsexual, solo.
   - Examples: shoulder_width, waist_width, muscle_tone.

6. Pose and posture sliders
   - Useful but can alter composition.
   - Start with simple posture differences before action poses.
   - Examples: posture_uprightness, head_tilt, pose_dynamism.

7. Background sliders
   - Good for safe experiments.
   - Keep the character prompt fixed and change only background terms.
   - Examples: background_detail, background_depth, indoor_outdoor.

8. Color and lighting sliders
   - Easy to evaluate visually.
   - Do not make one side low quality.
   - Examples: color_saturation, brightness, color_temperature.

9. Lineart and rendering sliders
   - Useful for style control.
   - Avoid low quality, bad anatomy, blurry as the opposite side.
   - Examples: line_weight, shading_complexity, cel_shading.

10. Abstract impression sliders
   - Do not use directly.
   - Decompose cute, beautiful, cool, elegant, mature into visible sub-concepts.

結論としては、最初に充実させるべきカテゴリは 表情・顔パーツ、髪、服装、色調、背景、線画 です。
その後に、身体比率・ポーズ・抽象印象の分解系を追加すると、Local LLM が「作りやすい slider」と「危ない slider」をかなり安定して見分けられるようになります。
